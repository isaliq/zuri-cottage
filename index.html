Gateway to Pahalgam

Adventures & Valley Wonders at Your Doorstep

Positioned conveniently in Lidroo, Zuri Cottages is the ideal launching pad for the most celebrated sights in Jammu & Kashmir.

0 km (At Doorstep)

Lidder River & Trout Angling

Step directly out behind the cottage to the pristine Lidder River. Enjoy morning strolls on the stone banks, river rafting in season, and world-renowned trout fishing.

8 km (~15 mins)

Betaab Valley

Named after the Bollywood film, this surreal valley features sprawling meadows surrounded by snow-dusted Himalayan peaks and crystalline mountain brooks.

6 km (Pony Trek)

Baisaran "Mini Switzerland"

A picturesque hilltop meadow fringed by dense deodar and pine forests, offering zorbing, pony rides, and breathtaking valley viewpoints.

13 km (~25 mins)

Aru Valley & Overa Biosphere

A pristine eco-hamlet serving as the base camp for Kolahoi Glacier and Tarsar Marsar alpine lakes, famous for scenic picnics and horse trekking.

16 km (~35 mins)

Chandanwari & Snow Sledging

The official starting point of the holy Shri Amarnath Yatra pilgrimage, known for year-round snow bridges, snow sledging, and dramatic canyon views.

4 km (~10 mins)

Pahalgam 18-Hole Golf Course

One of the highest altitude golf courses in the world, surrounded by rolling hills and ancient pine woods, located just a short drive from our gates.

Resort Policies

Check-In & Check-Out

Check-in: 02:00 PM | Check-out: 11:00 AM. Early check-in and late check-out can be accommodated subject to cottage availability.

Government ID Verification

Valid government IDs (Passport, Aadhaar Card, Driving License, or Voter ID) are required for all adult guests upon arrival. Primary guest must be at least 18 years of age.

Food & Meal Options

Fresh fixed menu and buffet dining available on property. Outside food is permitted. Food delivery apps do not operate in Pahalgam valley.

Pet & Smoking Policy

To preserve alpine hygiene and wood safety, smoking inside bedrooms is strictly prohibited (outdoor garden smoking designated). Pets are not allowed.
